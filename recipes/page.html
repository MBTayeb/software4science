The word 'algorithm' comes from Algorithmi, the Latinized form of Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi's name (c. 780–850). In software engineering, the term refers to a concrete recipe that solves a problem in a finite number of steps. It's the essence of software engineering. Especially in RSE, where most code consists of calculations and plots.

Because of the importance of algorithms in our code base, we want to make sure that they are of a high quality and efficiency. Analyzing this in a scientific manner was achieved with the big-oh notation. Its a "qualitative" but correct and allgemeingueltig.
